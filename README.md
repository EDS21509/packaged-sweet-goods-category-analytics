## 📌 Project Overview
Analyzed a 52-week Nielsen scan dataset (200 records) across major retail channels (Walmart, Kroger, Target, Convenience) to evaluate category market share, trade promotion efficiency, SKU velocity, and regional performance dynamics. 

Designed an executive-grade dashboard to translate raw syndicated scan data into actionable insights for commercial leadership.

## 🔑 Key Strategic Takeaways

### 1. Market Dominance
* **Category Share:** Little Debbie leads the Packaged Sweet Goods category with **73.9% dollar share** ($3.22M) and **81.1% unit volume share**.
* **SKU Velocity:** The top 5 SKUs account for **60.8% of brand sales**, led by **Swiss Rolls** ($574.7k) and **Oatmeal Cream Pies** ($470.9k).

### 2. Trade Promotion Optimization
* **Feature & Display Lift:** Merchandising displays generated **$174,243 in incremental revenue** ($9,680/week average lift).
* **Tactical Efficiency:** Feature & Display outperformed standard price reductions by **41.2%** (generating **$50,837 more** in incremental sales), proving that high-visibility end-cap placements drive significantly higher ROI than price cuts alone.

### 3. Geographic Concentration
* **Regional Stronghold:** The **Southeast region** drives **71.2% of total category sales** ($3.1M), with Walmart representing the largest volume driver ($1.86M in SE).
* **Expansion Opportunity:** Western and Northeastern markets combined account for under 12% of total category revenue, signaling significant whitespace for ACV distribution expansion.
